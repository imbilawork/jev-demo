What the independent tests say to keep in mind

  • Threshold on the maximum probability, not the confidence field. Calibrate per question on your own traffic.
  • Yes/no answers ran underconfident, choice and score ran overconfident, on the same inputs. One global threshold is unsafe.
  • The model does not know what it cannot see. Put organisational rules in the state or in code, and give every choice a none_of_these option.
  • Pin a model version before tuning thresholds. Send only the fields the question needs.

Source: The Decision Layer, a CTO briefing on know.2nth.ai.
